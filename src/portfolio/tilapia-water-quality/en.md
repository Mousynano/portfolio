## Collaboration scope

This submitted study explores sensor monitoring and fuzzy logic for interpreting water-quality conditions in tilapia cultivation. The embedded implementation and primary system development were led by another author, so this page focuses on my research and publication contribution.

## My contribution

My documented work covered conceptual framing of the paper, research-design review, reference validation, manuscript conversion and editing, and project administration.

The contribution helped turn a technical prototype into a more structured research argument: what operational gap it addresses, how the study is framed, whether references support the claims, and how the manuscript is prepared for submission.

## Why it matters to my portfolio

The project demonstrates work across engineering and research communication without claiming ownership of hardware or firmware developed by collaborators. It also reinforces a recurring theme in my work: technical systems become more useful when their assumptions, evidence, and operational purpose are clearly explained.
