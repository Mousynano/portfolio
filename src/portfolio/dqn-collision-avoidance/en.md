## Collaboration scope

This draft research compares DQN, DDQN, and D3QN approaches for collision avoidance. I present it as a research collaboration because the optimization framework and main software implementation were developed by another contributor.

## My contribution

My documented role covered program testing, reproducibility preparation, technical manuscript writing, and copy editing. That work included checking implementation behavior, helping make the experimental description reproducible, and improving how the results and limitations were communicated.

## Why it belongs here

Engineering work is not always sole ownership of a product. Testing, reproducibility, and technical communication are meaningful contributions when their boundaries are explicit. This page documents those boundaries rather than inflating them into a claim that I built the full reinforcement-learning system.

## Status

The manuscript remains a draft. Results should therefore be read as ongoing research rather than a published benchmark or deployed collision-avoidance product.
