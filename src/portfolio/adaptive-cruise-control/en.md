## Research question

Adaptive cruise control must keep a useful following distance without producing unstable or uncomfortable responses. This study asks how five metaheuristic algorithms perform when tuning a PID controller under multiple objective functions and safety-oriented evaluation.

## What was evaluated

The study compares Genetic Algorithm, Particle Swarm Optimization, Komodo Mlipir Algorithm, Sine-KM Arithmetic Optimization, and Hippopotamus Optimization Algorithm. The evaluation includes multiple independent runs, convergence behavior, stability, robustness, time response, computational efficiency, and time-to-collision safety scenarios.

## My contribution

My documented contribution includes methodology, simulation software, optimization framework implementation, formal analysis, visualization, and writing the original manuscript draft.

This is the research project where my ownership is broad enough to present the technical method and results directly, rather than only describing a collaboration role.

## Result

Particle Swarm Optimization produced the strongest overall balance in the reported experiments. KMA and SKA remained competitive, while all evaluated controllers were assessed across stability and safety scenarios rather than only one objective value.

## Status and limitation

The manuscript is submitted. The findings are based on simulation and should not be interpreted as a road-ready vehicle controller. Physical-system validation, sensor noise, actuator limits, and broader traffic scenarios remain necessary before deployment.
